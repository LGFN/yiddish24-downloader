YIDDISH24 DOWNLOADER - HOW TO INSTALL
=====================================

This adds a download button to every audio track on yiddish24.com.
It works in Google Chrome (and other Chrome-based browsers like Edge or Brave).


INSTALL (takes about a minute)
------------------------------

1. Unzip the file you received. You'll get a folder called "yiddish24-downloader".

2. Move that folder somewhere permanent, for example your Documents folder.
   Chrome loads the extension from this folder every time it starts,
   so don't delete or move it after installing.

3. Open Chrome and type this into the address bar, then press Enter:

       chrome://extensions

4. Turn on "Developer mode" (the switch in the top-right corner).

5. Click "Load unpacked" (top-left) and select the "yiddish24-downloader" folder.
   (Select the folder itself, the one that has manifest.json inside it.)

6. Done. Go to https://www.yiddish24.com and refresh the page.


HOW TO USE
----------

- Look for the small download icon (a circle with a down arrow) next to each
  track's share button. On the homepage, the buttons appear when you hover
  over a card.
- Click it to download. A ring around the icon shows the progress.
- Click it again while it's downloading to cancel.
- The icon turns green when the file is saved to your Downloads folder.


CAR STEREO / OLD MP3 PLAYER?
----------------------------

Some car stereos skip or show the wrong time on these files. Click the
extension's icon in Chrome's toolbar (the puzzle piece, then
"Yiddish24 Downloader") and turn on "Compatibility mode". Downloads will then
be converted into a format that older players handle well. This takes a
little longer.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------

- Chrome may sometimes show a message about "developer mode extensions".
  That's normal for extensions installed this way; just close it
  (don't click "Disable").
- To update to a newer version: replace the folder's contents with the new
  files, then click the circular reload arrow on the extension's card
  in chrome://extensions.
- To remove it: go to chrome://extensions and click "Remove".
